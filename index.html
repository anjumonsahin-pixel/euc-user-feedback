One thing to watch

The app keeps asking about Bluetooth

The same complaint turned up in two countries this month. On the Australian App Store one user said the app asks to add Bluetooth devices several times a week. On the German App Store another user raised the same nagging behaviour. It is a small thing on its own, but it is the one issue that crossed markets in August, so it is worth a look from the app team.

Juniper by market

The same brand, four different pictures

Juniper runs in four countries. Use the market filters in the menu to focus on one. Australia is the strongest, Germany the weakest, and Japan we could not read this month.

Australia AU

Trustpilot 4.5 · App Store 4.6 · ProductReview 4.4 · Google Play 4.6

The busiest and happiest market. August reviews praised the health coaches and the ease of booking and delivery. The two soft spots were price, mentioned in passing even in five star reviews, and getting hold of a person when the app is the first line of help.

United Kingdom UK

Trustpilot 4.5 · App Store 4.6 · reviews.io 3.5

Better covered this month, now that the UK Trustpilot page is readable. It sits at 4.5 across about 490 reviews, and August feedback was mostly warm, with repeated praise for the patient support team. The exception was one serious billing complaint: a reviewer said that after they reported financial hardship, support pressed them for an exit fee and threatened to contact their bank, and they took it to Trading Standards.

Germany DE

App Store 2.7

The one market that stands out for the wrong reasons. The German app score is low, and August reviews were about the app itself: the Bluetooth nagging, being asked to update almost daily, and one user who could not log in or even select Germany as their country.

Japan JP

No public score we could read

A blank this month. Juniper is newer in Japan and we found no public review page we could read. This is a gap to close, not a sign that Japan is quiet.

For the clinical team

Worth a closer look

Nothing new about side effects or safety came up in August. A few older reviews, from the UK App Store late last year and Germany over the summer, mention not being able to log injection doses in the app. That has a medical angle, so it is passed to the clinical team to judge. This page is a heads-up, not the formal safety report.
